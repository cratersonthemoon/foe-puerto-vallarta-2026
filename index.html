Grocery List
Coordinate with the concierge before arrival — they can source everything locally
🍸 The Villa Bar
  • Tequila & Mezcal — High-quality Blanco for cocktails (like Siete Leguas) and a Reposado or Añejo for sipping. A bottle of smoky Mezcal (like 400 Conejos).
  • Raicilla — The local spirit of Puerto Vallarta. Ask the concierge for a bottle of Hacienda El Divisadero.
  • Mixers & Fresh Fruit — Limes, grapefruit (for Palomas), agave nectar, and sparkling water (Topo Chico).
  • Local Beer — A mix of Pacifico (local favorite) and craft options like Cervecería Los Muertos.
👨‍🍳 The Chef's Kitchen
  • Fresh Seafood — Huachinango (Red Snapper) for a Zarandeado-style dinner and fresh shrimp for aguachile or ceviche.
  • Breakfast Staples — High-quality Oaxacan cheese, fresh avocados, tortillas (flour and corn), and local tropical fruits (mango, papaya, pitaya).
  • Snack Request — Guacamole & Pico de Gallo ingredients ready for the mid-day pool snack.
